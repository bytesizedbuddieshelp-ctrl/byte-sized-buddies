# Teaching guide: running a visit

How to run one 45-minute Byte-Sized Buddies lesson at a senior home, with the website's tools. The public version for other volunteers is the **Teach it yourself** page (`src/content/teach-it-yourself.md`). This one is for you, the owner, and adds the tech side.

## Before the first visit to a new home

- Reply to their request from **Admin, Contact exchange** and agree on a day, a time, and a room.
- Ask for one **staff contact person**. Get their name and the best way to reach them on the day.
- Ask: is there a TV or projector? Which cable does it take (usually HDMI)? Is there Wi-Fi, and what is the guest password?
- Ask roughly how many learners, and which devices they use. Bring that many handouts, plus two.
- Ask the facility how they handle photo consent. **No photos or video of learners without written consent from the person and the facility.** If you don't have it, don't take the picture.

## Arrive 15 minutes early

1. Set up the laptop and TV. Open **Present** and the audience window (see `docs/ADMIN-GUIDE.md`, "The day of the visit").
2. Connect your phone remote.
3. Put a handout and worksheet at each seat, face down.
4. Check the slides are readable from the back row. If not, move the chairs closer.

## The 45 minutes

| Time | Part | What you do | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 min | **Welcome** | Say your name. Ask the warm-up question from the teacher guide. Learn people's names. | Title slide. Press **Start the clock**. |
| 3 to 13 min | **Learn it** | One idea per slide. Show it once on the screen, slowly. Say what will happen next. | Phone remote: **Next**. Notes on your phone. |
| 13 to 33 min | **Try it** | Everyone tries on their own device. Walk around. Sit beside people. | Try it slide. Start the practice timer from the phone. **Blank** the screen if it distracts. |
| 33 to 40 min | **Worksheet and teach-back** | Each learner shows a neighbor what they learned. | Recap slide. |
| 40 to 45 min | **Keep it** | Turn over the handout. Read the take-home challenge together. Thank everyone. | Keep it slide. The clock warns you at 5 minutes left. |

## How to talk

- Short sentences. One action per sentence. "Tap the green phone icon. It opens your calls."
- Say what will happen next. "A box will pop up. That's normal."
- Make mistakes normal. "Everyone gets stuck here. Let's try again."
- Use people's names, or "older adults". Never "elderly".
- Explain a technical word the first time you use it, or don't use it.
- Never take over someone's device without asking. Point instead: "Try the button at the bottom."

## Safety rules (every lesson)

- **Never ask anyone to type a real password in class.** Use practice accounts or a practice screen.
- **The scam rule, calmly:** 1. Hang up, or don't click. 2. Call the number on your card. 3. Call someone you trust.
- Don't collect learners' personal details, phone numbers, or account information. The website is built not to store any.
- If someone shares a worry about a real scam or a real account, help them tell a staff member or family member. Don't log in to their accounts yourself.

## Hard moments

| What happens | What to say or do |
|---|---|
| Someone is far behind | "Let's do this one together." Sit beside them. The others can keep practicing. |
| Someone is far ahead | Ask them to help a neighbor, or try the take-home challenge early. |
| A device needs an update or a password nobody knows | Note it. Ask the staff contact to help after class. Move on with a practice screen. |
| The Wi-Fi or the TV fails | Keep going with the printed handout. Present from the laptop screen alone. |
| Someone is upset or frustrated | Slow down. "This is new for everyone. You're doing fine." Offer a break. |

## After the visit

1. Write down what worked and what was tricky, while you remember. Improve the kit for next time (re-import it with **Replace this lesson**).
2. Thank the staff contact by email, and confirm the next date (**Contact exchange**).
3. If a learner asked a question you couldn't answer, write it up as a ticket yourself and answer it later, with a short video if it helps.
