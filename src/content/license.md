Our lessons are free. You may print them, change them, and share them. Most lessons use the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** license, called CC BY-SA 4.0. Each lesson page shows its license, in case one is different.

## What you may do

- Download, print, and copy the lessons in any format.
- Adapt them for your learners, your language, or your devices.
- Share them with anyone, including in your own classes.

## What we ask in return

- **Give credit.** Say where the lesson came from.
- **Say what you changed.** A short note is enough.
- **Share alike.** If you share your changed version, use the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

## How to credit us

Copy this line and fill in the lesson name:

> "[Lesson name]" from Byte-Sized Buddies, [YOUR WEBSITE], used under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Read the full license at [creativecommons.org](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This page is a short summary, not the license itself.

## The name and the logo

The Byte-Sized Buddies name and logo are not part of the license. Please do not use them for a different project. If you teach our lessons, tell people the lessons come from Byte-Sized Buddies. Do not say your class is Byte-Sized Buddies.
