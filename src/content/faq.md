Short answers to what people ask us most. If yours isn't here, you can ask it on the [Ask a question](/ask) page.

## About Byte-Sized Buddies

### Who are you?

We are a student-led volunteer project. We teach basic technology to older adults in senior homes, and we share every lesson for free with anyone in the world.

### What is a "byte-sized" lesson?

One small, useful skill, taught in about 45 minutes. You leave able to do one real thing you could not do before, like making a video call or sharing a photo.

### Does it cost anything?

No. Visits, lessons, handouts, and slides are free, always. We do not take payments or donations on this site.

## For senior homes

### What do we need to provide?

A room, a screen or TV if you have one, Wi-Fi if you have it, and a staff contact person. We bring everything else.

### What happens during a visit?

A visit is one lesson, about 45 minutes. We welcome everyone, show a short slideshow, then spend most of the time on guided practice. Each person takes home a large-print handout. See [For senior homes](/for-senior-homes) for the full plan.

### How do we ask for a visit?

Fill in the form on the [For senior homes](/for-senior-homes#contact) page. We reply within two days.

### What devices do learners use?

Usually an iPhone, an Android phone, a tablet, or a computer. Tell us what your residents use in the form. If you are not sure, choose "Not sure".

### Do learners need an account?

No. Learners do not sign up for anything. Staff do not need an account either.

## Safety and privacy

### Do learners type real passwords?

Never. We use practice accounts in class, so no one types a real password.

### Do you take photos or video?

Only with written consent from the person and from your facility. No photos of learners are on this site unless that consent was given. You can read the [consent form we use](/consent-form).

### What information do you collect?

Only what you type into our two forms. Please don't include passwords, medical details, or bank information. Our [Privacy page](/privacy) explains what we keep, who can see it, and for how long. We use no advertising and no tracking.

### What if a learner gets a strange call or message?

Our [Stay safe online](/stay-safe) page has three calm steps: hang up, call the number on your card, and call someone you trust.

## For teachers and volunteers

### Can I teach these lessons myself?

Yes. Every lesson has a teacher guide, a slideshow, a worksheet, and a handout. Start with [Teach it yourself](/teach), then pick a lesson from the [library](/lessons). A [visit-day checklist](/visit-checklist) is there to print.

### Can I change the lessons?

Yes. Lessons use the Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0 license. You may print, adapt, and share them. Please credit Byte-Sized Buddies, and share your changes under the same license. Read the [License page](/license) for the details.

### Can I use your logo?

The name and logo are not part of the license. Please do not use them for a different project.

### How do I tell you how a lesson went?

Use the [contact form](/for-senior-homes#contact). We would love to hear it.

## Using this site

### Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. Use the **Text size**, **High contrast**, and **Roomy spacing** buttons at the top of every page. The site remembers your choice on your device. Lessons and answers also have a **Read this aloud** button.

### Can I print a lesson?

Yes. Every lesson page has a print button, and the slides can be saved as a PDF.
