We collect very little. We use it to answer you, and we keep it only as long as we need it.

## What we collect

When you use the **Request a visit** form or the **Ask a question** form, we collect what you type. That can include your name, your facility, your email, your phone number, the devices your learners use, the times that work for you, and your message or question.

The question form also makes a private link where you can read your answer.

Please don't type passwords, medical details, or bank information. Please don't send personal information about residents. We never ask for it.

## Why we collect it

To write back to you, to plan a visit, and to answer your question.

## Who can see it

Only the student who runs Byte-Sized Buddies. Nobody else has access to your form answers.

## How long we keep it

- Questions that have been answered or closed are deleted after about 90 days.
- Copies of emails in our inbox are deleted after about 90 days.
- Visit requests are kept only as long as we need them to plan a visit.

## No advertising and no tracking

This website has no advertising and no tracking. We do not use analytics. We do not use chat widgets or social media buttons.

## Services that help us run this site

- **Supabase** stores what you send through the forms.
- **Cloudflare** hosts the website.
- **Google** sends and receives our email through a Gmail account.
- **YouTube** shows our videos. We use its privacy-friendly player, youtube-nocookie.com. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press Play.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete what you sent. Use the [Ask a question](/ask) page and tell us what you would like removed.
