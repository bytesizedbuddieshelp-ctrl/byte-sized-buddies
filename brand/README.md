# Byte-Sized Buddies

**Learn it. Try it. Keep it.**

Byte-Sized Buddies is a volunteer project that teaches basic technology to older adults in senior homes, one patient, hands-on lesson a week. Everything we make is free for anyone to use and teach.

This is the brand book. Read it before making a slide, worksheet, handout, web page, or post. Colors, type, spacing and the logo are in the tokens and assets of this system.

## Mission

> Free, patient, hands-on technology lessons for older adults, and free materials for anyone who wants to teach them.

**Promise:** you leave every session able to do one real thing you couldn't do before.

**Who we speak to**

| Audience | What they need from us |
|---|---|
| Older adults (learners) | To feel respected, unhurried, and safe to make mistakes. |
| Senior home staff and activity directors | To trust us: organized, reliable, safe, no cost, no hassle. |
| Volunteers and teachers worldwide | Clear, ready-to-use materials they can download, adapt, and share. |

## The name

**Byte-Sized Buddies** plays on "bite-sized": small pieces that are easy to take in, and "byte", a piece of technology. "Buddies" says we learn side by side. Always write it **Byte-Sized Buddies**: capital B on each word, a hyphen in Byte-Sized, never "BSB" in public copy.

Before you print or register anything, check the domain, the social handles, and a trademark search for "Byte-Sized Buddies" in your country. Short forms like `bytesizedbuddies` and `bytesized-buddies` are the usual handles to try.

## Content fundamentals

### Voice: a patient neighbor

Warm, plain, and respectful. We explain like a good neighbor, not a help-desk script and not a cartoon.

**Do**
- Use short sentences and everyday words. "Tap the green phone icon. It opens your calls."
- Name one action per sentence.
- Normalize mistakes. "Everyone gets stuck here. Let's try again."
- Say "older adults" or use people's names. Say "learners" in volunteer materials.
- Tell people what will happen next. "A video will pop up. That's normal."

**Don't**
- Say *easy, simple, just, obviously,* or *basically*. If it isn't easy for someone, those words say they're the problem.
- Use jargon without a plain-language explanation the first time ("the cloud" becomes "stored online, not on your phone").
- Use baby talk, exclamation-mark pileups, or slang.
- Call anyone "elderly," "seniors" in headlines aimed at them, or "tech illiterate."
- Use fear to teach. Scam lessons teach calm rules ("Hang up. Call the number on your card.").

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Just tap the icon to easily open the app. | Tap the green phone icon. It opens your calls. |
| Utilize the search functionality. | Type your question in the white box at the top. |
| You should know this by now. | Many people find this step tricky. Let's go slowly. |
| Seniors can learn tech too! | Everyone can learn at their own pace. |

### Casing and punctuation
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. "Request a visit", not "Request A Visit".
- Numbers as digits in steps ("Step 1"). Spell out one to nine in running text.
- One idea per slide, one task per handout step.
- Tagline is written with three short sentences and periods: **Learn it. Try it. Keep it.**

### How the tagline works in a lesson
- **Learn it:** the slides. Short, one idea each.
- **Try it:** the hands-on practice and the worksheet.
- **Keep it:** the handout and the take-home challenge.

See `copy-bank.md` for ready-to-use sentences for the website, emails, and social profiles.

## Visual foundations

### Feel
Warm, calm, and legible. Soft shapes (we echo the logo's rounded corners), generous space, big type, and strong contrast. If a choice looks pretty but is hard to read, we choose readable.

### Color
The palette is **Garden**: a forest green, a sunshine yellow, a warm cream, and a charcoal ink.

- **forest** `#2F5D50` is the brand. Buttons, headings, links, the logo.
- **sunshine** `#FFD166` is the spark. Use it for highlights, badges and the logo crumb. It is never text on a light background.
- **cream** `#FAF7F0` is the default background everywhere. White is only for cards and worksheet answer areas.
- **ink** `#222222` is body text.
- Tints (`forest-tint`, `sunshine-soft`) give soft panels. `brick` is only for warnings, always with words and an icon.

Contrast rules (all checked): body text on cream is 14:1. White on forest is 7.5:1. Ink on sunshine is 11:1. **Do not put sunshine text on cream or white.** Colors that carry meaning must also be said in words, because some readers cannot tell them apart.

### Type
- **Fraunces** (soft, friendly serif) for titles and the wordmark.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (designed by the Braille Institute for readability) for everything people actually read.
- Website body text is 20px or larger. Smallest allowed text on screen is 18px. Slides are 40px or larger on a 1920x1080 canvas. Printed steps are 15pt or larger and never below 14pt.
- Left-align. No all caps paragraphs, no italics for long text, no light weights, no gray text lighter than `ink-muted`.

### Space and shape
- Generous margins: 48px on slides, 24px minimum card padding.
- Corners: `radius-md` for buttons and cards, `radius-lg` for panels and slides. Never sharp boxes.
- Buttons are at least 56px tall and 160px wide, with a clear label. Tap targets are never smaller than 48px.
- Flat paper look: a 2px `rule` shadow under cards. No blurry shadows, no gradients, no glass.
- Every interactive element has a visible `focus` ring.

### Imagery
Photos of real learners and volunteers appear only with written consent from the person and the facility. Prefer clear screenshots, close-ups of hands on devices, and large annotated arrows. Always add alt text.

## Logo

The mark is a friendly screen with a bite taken out of its corner (the "byte") and a sunshine crumb. Use the supplied files; never redraw or retype them.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `byte-sized-buddies-logo-horizontal.svg` | Website header, slide title slide, letterhead. |
| `byte-sized-buddies-logo-stacked.svg` | Posters, flyers, social profile cards. |
| `byte-sized-buddies-mark.svg` | Small spaces: social avatar, footers, slide corner. |
| `byte-sized-buddies-mark-reversed.svg` and `...-logo-horizontal-reversed.svg` | On forest green or other dark backgrounds. |
| `byte-sized-buddies-mark-mono.svg` | One-color printing and stamps. |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab icon. |

**Rules**
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of the crumb on all sides.
- Minimum size: mark 32px wide, horizontal logo 160px wide.
- Use the reversed versions on dark backgrounds only.
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, add shadows, or put the logo on busy photos.

## Iconography
Icons are large, outlined, 2.5px strokes, round caps, never filled with gradients. Each icon has a visible text label next to it: icons alone are not enough for new learners. Draw phone controls the way they look on the real device (the green phone, the camera, the speech bubble) so learners recognize them.

## Accessibility is the brand
- Text contrast of 4.5:1 or higher everywhere; 7:1 for body text where possible.
- Don't rely on color alone. Don't use motion that can't be stopped.
- The site works with the keyboard alone and with a screen reader.
- Provide every worksheet and handout as a large-print PDF.
- Test the website at 200% zoom.
