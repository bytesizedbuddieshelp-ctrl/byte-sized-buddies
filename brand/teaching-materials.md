# Teaching materials: templates and lesson structure

## A weekly kit

Every lesson ships with the same six pieces. Keep the same names, order and look every week.

| Piece | Format | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher guide | PDF or web page | A 45-minute, minute-by-minute script with common mistakes and what to say. |
| Slideshow | 12 to 15 slides, 16:9 | `forest-deep` or `cream` backgrounds, one idea per slide. |
| Worksheet | Large-print PDF, 1 page | Fill-ins and "try it" tasks, with an answer key for the teacher only. |
| Handout | Large-print PDF, 1 page | Numbered steps with a real screenshot. The "Keep it" page. |
| Take-home challenge | A line on the handout | One small task to try during the week. |
| Video (optional) | 2 to 3 minutes | Opener, screen demo, closer. |

## Lesson flow (45 minutes)
1. Welcome and a warm-up question (3 min)
2. **Learn it:** slides (10 min)
3. **Try it:** guided practice (20 min)
4. Worksheet and teach-back to a neighbor (7 min)
5. **Keep it:** hand out the handout; explain the take-home challenge (5 min)

## Slide template (1920 x 1080)
- Margins 48px (`space-6`). Corners `radius-lg`.
- Title slide: `forest-deep` background, reversed logo, title in `slide-title` cream, a sunshine bar.
- Content slides: `cream` background, title in forest, body in `slide-body`. One idea. Fewer than 25 words.
- Step slides: number in a sunshine circle, step in `slide-step`, real screenshot at least half the slide.
- Practice slides: a `sunshine-soft` panel labeled **Try it** at the top.
- Footer: the mark (32px) and the lesson name, 28px minimum.

## Worksheet template (US Letter, print)
- Margins 0.75in. Title in `print-title`, forest.
- Each question in `print-step` with at least 0.5in of answer space.
- Maximum 6 tasks. Use checkboxes for "I did it".
- Answer area in white (`paper`) with a `rule` border.
- Footer in `print-note`: "Byte-Sized Buddies · Free to print and share".

## Handout template (US Letter, print)
- One page. The title says what the learner can now do ("Calling a Friend").
- 4 to 6 numbered steps, each with a real, cropped screenshot and a short sentence.
- A **Remember** box in `forest-tint`.
- A **This week's challenge** box in `sunshine-soft`.
- A "Need help?" line with how to ask a family member or staff.

## Safety rules in every kit
- Never ask learners to enter a real password in class. Use demo accounts or a practice screen.
- Include the scam-safety reminder: hang up, don't click, call someone you trust.
- Photos or video of learners only with written consent.
