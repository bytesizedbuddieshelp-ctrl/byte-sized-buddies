**Hero subhead:** Free, patient, hands-on technology lessons for older adults, and free materials for anyone who wants to teach them.

**How a visit works**
1. **Learn it.** We start with a short, friendly slideshow. One idea at a time.
2. **Try it.** Everyone practices on their own device, with help beside them.
3. **Keep it.** Each person takes home a large-print handout to remember what they learned.

**For senior homes**
We bring everything. You provide a space, a TV or projector if you have one, and a few residents who want to learn. There's no cost, ever. Lessons run about 45 minutes. Each lesson comes with a large-print handout for every learner.

**What we need from you:** a room, a screen or TV (optional), Wi-Fi if available, and a staff contact person.
